Delete your Tawari account

Tawari is a living field guide to Colombia's biodiversity, an app by Martin Smit. Here is how to delete your account and your data, whether or not the app is still on your phone.

In the app

  1. Open Tawari and go to Profile › Data & Privacy.
  2. At the end of that page, tap "Delete My Account" and confirm with "Delete".

If you signed in with Apple, Apple asks you to confirm once more. Your account is deleted there and then, and the app goes back to the start. It also works if you never signed in with Google or Apple.

You need an internet connection. If you do not see the button, the app could not reach our server: try again when you are online, or write to us.

Without the app

Write to hello@tawari.id from the email of the Google or Apple account you signed in with, with the subject "Delete my Tawari account". You do not need to explain anything else. We answer within the periods in our privacy policy and write back when it is done.

If you never signed in with Google or Apple, your account has no email or name, and without the app we cannot tell which one is yours. While you still have the app, delete it from there. If you no longer have it, write to us anyway, with roughly when you used Tawari and your phone's model, and we will do our best to find it.

What is deleted

  • Your account, with your name and email.
  • Every record in My List, with its notes, place, date, the point you logged it from and its photos.
  • Your life list and your progress at each place and in the Field-finds.
  • If you signed in with Apple, we also ask Apple to remove Tawari's access to your Apple account.

What is kept, and why

Some data stays in our database, with no link to your account:

  • Usage events from the app, which tell us which parts of the guide are useful.
  • Your consultations of the identification wizard, which we use to measure whether it gets species right.
  • The reports you sent us about a species card, because a correction keeps helping everyone else. If you wrote a comment in one, that text is kept as you wrote it.

They have no automatic deletion period, as section 5 of the privacy policy says. The app's crash reports stay at Sentry, our monitoring provider, for as long as Sentry keeps them.

Delete only some data

You can delete a record and its photo without deleting your account: in My List, tap the record, then "Delete this record". For anything else, such as the text of a report about a card, write to hello@tawari.id.

If you also left your email on this site's waitlist, that email is separate from your account. Tell us in your message and we will delete it too.
