Project status

This is what is inside Tawari today. It lives here rather than on the front page because it only matters once you have decided it matters. Every figure came from a query against the production database on 20 September 2026, and changes with each release.

The catalogue

A "written" card went through full drafting and a review. A "draft" card says there is little verifiable public information about that species and shows only what is documented. The two are told apart at a glance in the app.

The places

WhatHow many
Published places25
Place–species rows, from real records20,875
Active Field-finds3, of 100 species each

The 25 places run from Tayrona National Natural Park to Puracé, from the Ciénaga Grande de Santa Marta to the botanical gardens of Bogotá, Medellín and Cali. The three Field-finds, by contrast, are all in Valle del Cauca: the Cali Botanical Garden, Cerro de las Tres Cruces and Sonso Lagoon.

That last column says the most. The Botanical Garden is well documented; Cerro de las Tres Cruces barely. Both lists hold a hundred species and they are not worth the same, and we would rather that showed.

"Showable photo" excludes the CC BY-NC and CC BY-NC-SA licences. It is not that photographs are missing: they exist, and we cannot show them, because an app with a paid tier is commercial use. We keep them without publishing them.

The explorer on the front page shows a few fewer: 89, 94 and 95 respectively. The difference is nine photographs whose licence did permit showing them, but whose origin server — herbaria at NYBG and the Jardim Botânico do Rio de Janeiro — did not answer when we built this version of the site. They will sort themselves out in the next one.

How they are counted
