How they are counted

Every figure is a count (COUNT(*)) against the production database, read-only. What each one counts:

  • Species in the catalogue: those you can find in the app. The database holds more that are not shown yet.
  • Written or draft card: the state each card has stored.
  • Openly licensed photo: CC0, public domain, CC BY or CC BY-SA, with no NC or ND mark.
  • Pairs of place and species: the species on each published place's list, drawn from GBIF and eBird records.
  • Species in a Field-find: those left after the ones we removed when reviewing each list.
  • Published relationships: those from manual curation, and those from GloBI that meet the evidence rule (several datasets or several records, with a record from Colombia or three independent datasets) or that we have reviewed and approved. Each card shows the strongest one in each of its three slots.
  • Pages and essays: those the Learn tab shows.

If you would like the exact queries, write to hello@tawari.id and we will send them. If a figure on this page does not match what you see in the app, this page is the one that is wrong: write to us and we will fix it.
