Tawari Privacy Policy

The Spanish version is the authoritative one; this English version is kept in step with it.

1. Who we are

Tawari is a living field guide to Colombia's biodiversity. This policy explains what we collect when you use the app and the tawari.id website, what we use it for, and what you can do about it.

The data controller is Martin Smit, acting as a natural person, domiciled at Edificio Altavista, Avenida 6 Oeste #5-200, Santiago de Cali, Valle del Cauca, Colombia. You can reach us at hello@tawari.id about anything in this policy.

2. What we collect and why

We collect the least we need to make the app work and to understand which parts of the guide are useful and which are not.

Account and session. You can use Tawari without creating an account. Even then we create an anonymous session with its own identifier, because that is what lets your sightings and progress be saved and protected as yours. If you choose to sign in with Google or Apple, we receive your email address and, when the provider supplies it, your name. We never receive or store your password.

Your records. Your sighting is saved in My List, at the place you pick, or at none of Tawari's places if you pick "None of these" (see "Location"). We store the species, that place, the date and time, your notes, and the photo you attach if you attach one. This is your content and it stays tied to your session. By default the Tawari team can see your sightings, to check identifications and improve the guide. You can switch this off with "Share with the Tawari team", when you log the sighting or later by editing it, except when you ask us to help identify the species: we cannot do that without seeing it.

Location. We ask for your location only while you are using the app, never in the background. We use it to centre the map where you are and to show you nearby places; that calculation happens on your phone. When you log a sighting, if you gave the permission and leave "Log location" on (it starts on), we also store the point you logged it from. If you do not pick a place (for example, with "None of these"), Tawari uses that point to look for the Tawari place or city you are in; if it finds none, or if you do not share your location, the sighting is saved without a place. You can decline the permission and the app still works.

Photos. If you attach a photo, the app resizes and re-compresses it on your device before uploading. It is kept in private storage and shown through time-limited links. It is not published on the open internet and is not reachable by search engines.

App usage. We record usage events to learn what works. Every event carries its name, the operating system, the app's language, the installed version, and your session identifier. Some add one more detail: which species card you open; the species and place of each sighting you log, whether it has a photo, and whether it is your first record of that species; the place you started the identification wizard from, if you started from one; the reason for a report about a card; the group and the count on an achievement card you open to share; and which provider you sign in with. These events are stored in our database on Supabase; we do not send them to any analytics service.

The identification wizard. For each consultation we store your answers, the species it suggested, the one you chose, and how long it took you, tied to your session and to a random identifier generated on your device. This measures whether the wizard is getting things right. It is not used to profile you.

Reports about a card. If you tell us something is wrong on a card, we store the reason you choose, your comment if you write one, the species, the place if you opened the card from one, and your session.

Crashes and errors. If the app crashes or runs slowly, we send a technical report to our monitoring provider, with your session identifier attached so we can reproduce the problem.

No advertising, no tracking. We use no advertising identifiers, sell no data, share nothing with advertisers, and embed no third-party tracking networks in the app.

The website and the waitlist. On tawari.id you can leave your email so we can tell you when your beta invitation is ready. We store your email, the country you indicate, the language you viewed the site in, and the date you accepted this policy. The form sends them straight to our database on Supabase, to a list that anyone can join but no one can read from the internet. We use it for one purpose only: sending you that notice. The site uses no cookies, analytics, or trackers. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which logs every visitor's IP address for security purposes. You can ask us to delete your email at any time by writing to hello@tawari.id.

3. Who we share data with

We work with providers that process data on our behalf and only to deliver their service to us:

ProviderPurpose
SupabaseDatabase (including the website's waitlist), authentication, photo storage
SentryCrash and performance reporting
Apple MapsMap rendering on iPhone
Google and AppleSign-in, if you choose to use it
GitHubHosting of the tawari.id website

Photos and maps from other sources. The guide's species photos load straight from the archives that publish them: mostly iNaturalist and Wikimedia Commons, and also museum and herbarium collections. A species' range map loads from GBIF. As with opening any web page, those servers receive your device's IP address and the technical details of the request; we do not send them your account or your records. If you tap "Open in Maps" on a place, we open Apple Maps or Google Maps with that place's coordinates, and from there that app's own policy applies.

We share your data with no one else, unless a competent authority requires it by law.

4. Where your data is processed

Our providers operate servers that may be located outside Colombia. By using Tawari you consent to that international transfer, carried out under each provider's contractual terms.

5. How long we keep it

We keep your sightings, photos, and account data for as long as your account exists. Usage events, wizard consultations, and reports about cards are stored in our database with no automatic deletion period; if you delete your account, they are kept with no link to it. Crash reports are kept for as long as Sentry keeps them. Your waitlist email is kept until you ask us to delete it.

Our databases remain in force for as long as Tawari is in operation. Within that period, each piece of data is kept for the time this section sets out.

6. Your rights

Under Colombia's Law 1581 of 2012 and its implementing rules (among them Decree 1377 of 2013, now part of Decree 1074 of 2015), you have the right to know, update, and correct your personal data; to request proof of the consent you gave; to be told how we use your data; to complain to the Superintendency of Industry and Commerce; to withdraw your consent at any time and ask us to delete your data, unless a legal or contractual duty requires us to keep it; and to access your personal data free of charge. If you are in a jurisdiction with comparable rights, such as the European Union or the United Kingdom, we honour equivalent requests.

Who handles requests, and how. Queries and complaints are handled by the data controller at hello@tawari.id. Write from the email tied to your account, or tell us how to identify your session.

  • Queries (what data we hold about you, or how we use it): we answer within 10 business days of receiving it. If we cannot meet that, we tell you why and when we will answer, which is never more than 5 business days after the first deadline.
  • Complaints (to correct, update, or delete your data, to withdraw your consent, or to report a breach): include your identification, a description of the facts, a contact address, and any documents you want to rely on. If something is missing, we ask for it within 5 days; if 2 months pass without it, we take the complaint as withdrawn. We answer within 15 business days, counted from the day after we receive it. If we cannot meet that, we tell you why and when we will answer, which is never more than 8 business days after the first deadline.

Before you complain to the Superintendency of Industry and Commerce, the law asks that you first bring the query or complaint to us.

Deleting your data. You can delete your account in the app (Profile › Data & Privacy › Delete My Account): we delete your account, your sightings, and your photos. Usage events, wizard consultations, and reports about cards are kept with no link to your account (see section 5). You can also ask by email to hello@tawari.id, and we answer within the periods in this section.

The steps, with or without the app, and what is deleted and what is kept: Delete your Tawari account.

7. Children

Tawari is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child in your care gave us data, write to us and we will delete it.

8. Security

Connections to our servers are encrypted. Access to your records is restricted to your own session by security rules in the database; the Tawari team sees the sightings you share with it (see "Your records"). As the database's administrators, we can also open other records and photos when something needs fixing, as the app tells you when you start. No system is infallible, but we handle your data with the same care we give the content of the guide.

9. Changes to this policy

This is version 1.2, in force since 13 October 2026. If we change something material, we will update this page and the effective date. If a change significantly affects how we use your data, we will tell you inside the app.
