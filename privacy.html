Tawari Privacy Policy

The Spanish version is the authoritative one; this English version is kept in step with it.

1. Who we are

Tawari is a living field guide to Colombia's biodiversity. This policy explains what we collect when you use the app, what we use it for, and what you can do about it.

The data controller is Martin Smit, acting as a natural person, domiciled at Edificio Altavista, Avenida 6 Oeste #5-200, Santiago de Cali, Valle del Cauca, Colombia. You can reach us at hello@tawari.id about anything in this policy.

2. What we collect and why

We collect the least we need to make the app work and to understand which parts of the guide are useful and which are not.

Account and session. You can use Tawari without creating an account. Even then we create an anonymous session with its own identifier, because that is what lets your sightings and progress be saved and protected as yours. If you choose to sign in with Google or Apple, we receive your email address and, when the provider supplies it, your name. We never receive or store your password.

Your records. When you log a sighting we store the species, the place you picked from our catalogue, the date, your notes, and the photo you attach if you attach one. This is your content and it stays tied to your session.

Location. We ask for your location only while you are using the app, never in the background. We use it to centre the map where you are and to show you nearby places. A sighting stores the place you select from our catalogue, not your exact coordinates. You can decline the permission and the app still works.

Photos. If you attach a photo, the app resizes and re-compresses it on your device before uploading. It is kept in private storage and shown through time-limited links. It is not published on the open internet and is not reachable by search engines.

App usage. We record usage events to learn what works: the event name, the operating system, the app's language, the installed version, and your session identifier. In the identification wizard we also store the questions and answers of each consultation, tied to a random identifier generated on your device. This measures whether the wizard is getting things right. It is not used to profile you.

Crashes and errors. If the app crashes or runs slowly, we send a technical report to our monitoring provider, with your session identifier attached so we can reproduce the problem.

No advertising, no tracking. We use no advertising identifiers, sell no data, share nothing with advertisers, and embed no third-party tracking networks in the app.

The website and the waitlist. On tawari.id you can leave your email so we can tell you when the pre-beta opens. We store your email, the country you indicate, the language you viewed the site in, and the date you accepted this policy. We use it for one purpose only: sending you that notice. The site uses no cookies, analytics, or trackers. You can ask us to delete your email at any time by writing to hello@tawari.id.

4. Where your data is processed

Our providers operate servers that may be located outside Colombia. By using Tawari you consent to that international transfer, carried out under each provider's contractual terms.

5. How long we keep it

We keep your sightings, photos, and account data for as long as your account exists. Usage data and crash reports are kept for shorter periods, according to each provider's settings.

6. Your rights

Under Colombia's Law 1581 of 2012 and its implementing rules, you have the right to know, update, and correct your personal data, to request proof of the consent you gave, to be told how we use your data, to complain to the Superintendency of Industry and Commerce, and to request deletion of your data where that applies. If you are in a jurisdiction with comparable rights, such as the European Union or the United Kingdom, we honour equivalent requests.

How to exercise them: write to hello@tawari.id from the email tied to your account, or tell us how to identify your session. We answer within the periods the law sets.

Deleting your data. Today, deletion is requested by email to hello@tawari.id and we process it by hand: we delete your account, your sightings, and your photos. We answer within the periods the law sets. In-app deletion is being built; until it exists, email is the route and we do service it.

7. Children

Tawari is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child in your care gave us data, write to us and we will delete it.

8. Security

Connections to our servers are encrypted. Access to your records is restricted to your own session by security rules in the database. No system is infallible, but we handle your data with the same care we give the content of the guide.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change something material, we will update this page and the effective date. If a change significantly affects how we use your data, we will tell you inside the app.
